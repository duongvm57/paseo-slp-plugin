# SLP work coordination

SLP assigns bounded work and independent judgment while retaining Human
authority and evidence-based acceptance.

## Language

**Assignment**:
A bounded objective, its authority and the owner responsible for its result.
_Avoid_: Agent session, spawn tree

**Operative brief**:
The current statement of an assignment's outcome, real constraints,
provisional choices, unknowns and required evidence.
_Avoid_: Final plan, implementation prescription

**Real constraint**:
A requirement backed by an identified authority, rather than an agent's
preferred design.
_Avoid_: Assumption, current design

**Decision**:
An owner's reasoned ruling on a material proposition, with evidence,
consequences and unresolved risks.
_Avoid_: Consensus, message acknowledgment

**Moving scope**:
Work whose changing surfaces and shared resources have one write owner.
_Avoid_: Workspace, agent identity

**State owner**:
The module responsible for a state or lifecycle, distinct from the agent
temporarily implementing a change.
_Avoid_: Agent owner

**Review mandate**:
The question an independent seat investigates on an identified candidate.
_Avoid_: Desired verdict, provider family

**Review decision**:
The owner's reasoned selection of independent mandates, or determination
that no review trigger applies, under the assignment's authority.
_Avoid_: Reviewer count, automatic risk score

**Review waiver**:
An explicitly authorized exception to an otherwise required review obligation.
_Avoid_: No review trigger, missing reviewer

**Handback**:
A bounded worker's result, evidence, discoveries and remaining work returned
to the assigned receiving owner.
_Avoid_: Acceptance, lifecycle completion

**Handoff**:
An explicit transfer of assignment context and moving ownership to a
receiving session, with old-owner settlement and recipient acknowledgment.
_Avoid_: Parent reassignment, context summary

**Settlement**:
The receiving owner's account of delivery, remaining rework and resource
dispositions.
_Avoid_: Acceptance, automatic cleanup

**Claim**:
An actor's account of work, authority or evidence, retained with its source.
_Avoid_: Observation, execution proof

**Observation**:
A measurement actually made by the runtime or verifier on identified state.
_Avoid_: Self-report, acceptance
