# Jev routing

Unreadable error blocks dependent routing; unconfigured/off needs no Jev path.
Jev is a typed decision primitive, not an agent. Ordinary slp_seat_create
obtains the decision server-side when runtime is omitted. Explicit CLI compatibility
invokes route-decide; admitted formation pins once, replay never re-decides;
prepare and --check verify receipts offline, without background loops.
Manager configuration/keys need Human authority.

Shadow evaluation is the gate before arming: Lead supplies its independent
option/pair. Human pre-registers agreement/asymmetric-error criteria and arms
once pairs qualify. Shadow is advisory; armed routing requires the receipt's
option, with decline failing closed. Confidence/distribution grants no retry threshold.

Runtime precomputes eligibility, withholds private notes and verifies
provider/model/semantics/question/role/hash/membership. V1 keeps token conflicts
and vocabulary; axis:value mentions remain unverified prose. V2 sends every
eligible preset's Human description/hints and supported effort pairs, including
custom entries. Names/origin/dispositions are not enums. Lead reads the same
projection; explicit rules/reference constraints filter before Jev. Preserve
thinkingOptionId/selectionSha256/routingContext; Armed binds the full pair,
without effort override. Missing identity/capability or oversized choices are
gaps, never guessed defaults.

Supplied receipts are checked even off: consistency, not cryptographic authenticity
or proof Jev answered. Toggle changes affect future preparation, not running seats.
Armed failure/decline returns to Human-owned pool/config decisions, without
substitution/search. Human may disable routing to resume Lead choice while
retaining the key; agents cannot grant degradation. Same-user processes can
read 0600 keys; daemon-home integrity is the boundary. Preserve pinned
provider/model/endpoint and honest outage/key/capability gaps.
