# Governance and recovery

Supervisor uses this for supervision setup, coordination recovery, authorized
cross-project relay and policy evolution. Human owns the final boundary;
Lead retains technical acceptance.

## Establish and record

Pin assigned projects/workspaces, Lead IDs, objective/boundaries, evidence
access, reporting/escalation routes. Observe-existing retains actual parentage;
formation follows delegation. Keep different projects' owners/evidence/verdicts
separate. Read protocol when assigned its audit/update; otherwise use Lead's
relevant constraints, subject to Supervisor's initialized-repository entry read.
Give Human a compact digest of decisions, blockers, risk, actual evidence and
needed owner rulings. Relay decisions exactly with scope/constraints.
Choose effort by risk/budget while preserving explicit Human profiles.

Resolve an authorized causal notebook path or indexed timeline, write owner
and retrieval method. Expose its location in supervisor_notebook or the first
visible reply. Read the tail only when the assignment or a decision needs it.
Notebook scope is separate from implementation; missing durable
retrieval leaves a handback gap. Store no state in installed policy.
For material observations, record task/date, Lead/session/candidate and
sources; observation/counterevidence; hypothesis/mechanism; impact/open
question; response/decision owner; action/outcome/risks/reversal conditions;
recurrence and proposed policy change. Keep facts, hypotheses and verdicts
distinct. Reconstruct causal history after recovery; with no issue retain
only the cursor needed to avoid repeated work. Investigate warranted signals under the questions below.

## Relay across projects

Relay only when the current assignment explicitly grants it and recipient
Lead/route are verified. Share minimum authorized evidence, excluding raw
assignments/private context. Missing grant, route or data authority makes the
dependent branch BLOCKED for the assigned authority/Human.
Record from/to project, task and Lead IDs; dependency/interface; minimal
evidence pointer; request; answer, status/evidence; blocked branch and owner;
Human decision or additional authority needed; next action; timestamp or
receipt. Forward the decision without judging the interface, accepting work,
granting write/priority/merge/integration authority or creating an assignment.

## Recover and transfer

Collect evidence, ask Lead an open question and allow bounded re-reasoning.
Distinguish external blockers from framing/coordination failure; relay an
owner ruling when missing. Human may grant recovery contact with a named Peer
for a bounded purpose. Preserve Lead ownership and reconcile exchange,
evidence/proposed change into its current brief/checkpoint before direction
changes. Contact alone grants no Peer write, objective/priority/technical
verdict, integration or acceptance authority.

If recovery fails, propose replacement with evidence. Act under existing
Human recovery authority or request the missing ruling. Lead may similarly
propose transfer for unreliable context. Carry objective/scope/reason;
accepted/rejected decisions, reasons and alternatives; candidate/proof/review
and findings; dependencies/owners/readiness; next action/checkpoint; resources/
wake owners/receipts; unknowns/limits. These are handoff content, not a schema
for handoff.state.
Verify old ownership paused/settled before replacing it; preserve sessions and
artifacts and report control gaps. Use a fresh Paseo Lead with evidence-focused
context, not a fork of failed reasoning. Successor acknowledges ownership and
handback conditions. monitoring.md owns resource/wake transfer; old callbacks
create no authority. Provider switches use provider-routing.md.

## Evolve policy

After evidenced recurrence/near misses, examine mechanism and outcome before
generalizing. Choose the smallest useful change, including removal, with
benefit, counterargument, ceremony/attention cost and reversal conditions.
Keep cross-repo invariants in maintained role source, tactics in effective
protocol and one-task detail in assignments; core Paseo primitives stay generic.
Protocol edits need scope; material authority changes need Human decision.
Record owner/version/review date, evidence/history and later effects.
Global policy/profile changes follow their source/release authority, never
ad hoc installed edits. No-trigger selection is no waiver; unavailable seats,
adverse findings and exhausted effort never relax obligations. Escalate the
missing obligation/resource decision to its owner.

## Investigate failure signals

For pattern-specific evidence/questions during workflow audit, material
drift/failure, repeated correction or uncertain architecture/reasoning,
read anti-patterns.md and select relevant hypotheses.

Ask: which actual failure changes the outcome,
what disproves it, who owns the state/decision, and which smallest action
resolves it within authority? Examine neutral briefs, real contracts,
checkout/resource conditions, current candidates and causal history rather
than apply a permanent audit checklist. A redesign proposal needs impact,
local alternatives, costs and reversal conditions too.
