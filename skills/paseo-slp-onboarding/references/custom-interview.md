# Custom process interview

Use only when Human requests a fully custom workflow. Ordinary setting changes
need no deep interview. Ask one question at a time in the Human's words; show
the relevant artifact and restate each decision.

1. What work arrives, with what risks? Map outcomes to recipes or a default
   with evidence/risk exceptions.
2. Which phases and evidence does each need? Identify authority and acceptance
   boundaries, delivery completion, work state and any justified Lead split.
3. Which additional judgment/artifact needs a seat? Offer relevant dispositions:
   Scout/Researcher/Analyst/Architect/Advisor for discovery/design; Engineer,
   Migrator, Integrator, Tech Writer or Prototyper for owned artifacts; Reviewer,
   domain Auditor or Proof Auditor for candidate/evidence checks. Map skills to
   chosen work. Lead retains decisions and verification; dispositions are not
   model assignments or a mandatory pipeline.

Installed authority, parentage and ownership invariants remain. A required gate
defaults to independent Spec + Standards seats; the protocol owns the shape.
Template choices (including requiring review for every recipe and the two-seat
gate) are defaults; any Human change must be explicit in Overrides with
decider/date, and single-seat classes must satisfy installed review-gate rules.
Keep defaults until changed. Extra cross-family review is optional; its
unavailability does not block or replace the declared gate.

Done: every named work kind has a recipe/gate, chosen seats have skills,
configuration fields are resolved or open, and the Human has seen the effective
protocol and knows it can be revised. Use onboarding's confirmed-write procedure.
